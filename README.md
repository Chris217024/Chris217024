# Hi, I'm Chris Khomo

I’m a Learning Experience Designer and the founder of **Equation Station SA**, a South African mathematics learning platform focused on making difficult concepts clear, visual and useful.

## What I build

- CAPS-aligned mathematics lessons and revision resources
- Interactive quizzes, diagnostic assessments and practice banks
- Visual explanations, animations and simulations
- Responsive learning experiences for desktop and mobile
- Structured learning journeys that connect concepts, examples and practice

## Featured work

### [Equation Station SA](https://equationstation-sa.pages.dev/)

A growing mathematics platform for South African learners, with topic-based lessons, worked examples, exam-focused practice and guided support.

### [Intelligent Investor Masterclass](https://github.com/Chris217024/intelligent-investor-masterclass)

A self-contained interactive learning experience that presents complex investing ideas through clear sequencing, visual hierarchy and accessible navigation. Created for educational and portfolio purposes.

## My approach

I combine mathematics teaching, learning design and web technology. My goal is not simply to place information online, but to help learners understand what to do, why it works and how to practise independently.

## Current focus

- Expanding Equation Station SA across the Grade 10–12 CAPS curriculum
- Improving interactive assessment and learner feedback
- Designing visual explanations for difficult mathematical ideas
- Building accessible, mobile-friendly educational resources
